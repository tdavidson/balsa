# Demo Lumenloop accounting policies

Synthetic owner-approved policy memo dated 2026-01-01. Accrual basis, USD, monthly reporting. The opening balance document is authoritative for December 31, 2025.

- Monthly service revenue is earned in its service month. Enterprise invoices create receivables until collected.
- Annual subscriptions are collected in advance, recorded as deferred revenue and recognized evenly over the 12 stated service months. Monthly MRR is annual contract value divided by 12. ARR is closing MRR times 12; neither is cash receipts.
- Stripe clearing records gross customer collections, fees, refunds and payouts. Each payout is a transfer from Stripe clearing into checking, never additional sales. Original processing fees are retained on refunds. No reserves, disputes or currency conversions exist in this simplified pack. Stripe exports are simplified test extracts, not a full provider schema or live API integration.
- Bank cash is checking plus savings; processor clearing is a separate current asset, excluded from bank cash in reference cash flows.
- Transfers between checking and savings are neither revenue nor expense. Founder contributions are equity financing.
- Annual insurance is prepaid April 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027, amortized $1,000 per month.
- The $2,400 laptop placed in service June 1 is depreciated $100 per month over 24 months with no residual value. Simplified demo policy uses a $2,000 capitalization threshold.
- Payroll is $30,000 gross plus $3,000 employer taxes monthly. Employee withholding is $6,000; net wages are $24,000 and tax remittances $9,000. Everything is paid within the service month. Do not add net wages, gross wages and withholding as separate expenses.
- Vendor services are expensed in their service month, including unpaid invoices. September's $1,800 security review is payable October 15.
- No annual renewal, proposed hire or budget is an approved new financial commitment without explicit review. Refund credits do not change MRR unless a subscription changes.
- Upload duplicates and summary reports must not create a second set of postings. Reference statements and the ledger are alternate representations of the same activity.
