# Demo Lumenloop

Entirely synthetic B2B SaaS company for Balsa demos. No real company or financial information is used.

Product: project handoff and client reporting software for small design studios.
Founded: 2026-01-01; opening founder contribution was received 2025-12-31.
Legal entity: one fictional US corporation. Currency: USD. Fiscal year: calendar year.
History: January through September 2026, all complete months. Initial review date: 2026-10-01.
Four employees; aggregate payroll only. No subsidiaries, debt, foreign currency, dividends or taxes payable in this simplified demo. Sales tax, income tax and interest are explicitly assumed zero.

Monthly plan: $1,500 per customer; cancel at month end. Customer 03 downgrades to $1,000 effective September 1. Annual plans are individually contracted.
Contacts: demo-founder@example.test and demo-finance@example.test. All account and provider IDs use demo_ prefixes and cannot authenticate to any service.

The demo distinguishes subscriptions, collections, recognized revenue, annual prepayments and bank transfers. September ends with a Stripe payout in transit and an unpaid enterprise invoice.
