# Monthly subscription terms

Synthetic standard agreement, effective January 1, 2026.
Demo Lumenloop charges $1,500 per customer per calendar service month, paid through Demo Pay; no setup fee or tax. A monthly plan may be cancelled before the next service month. The customer roster and invoice export identify actual participants and dates.

Demo Customer 02 cancels after July 31; no August or September service is due. Demo Customer 03 has an approved price reduction to $1,000 from September 1. Demo Customer 04 receives a one-time $500 September service credit, refunded September 4; its recurring price remains $1,500.

Customer 11 and Customer 12 have September payments processed September 30; all other monthly payments are processed on the first day in this simplified dataset. No late fee is imposed.
