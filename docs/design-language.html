Design language, approved 5 September 2026

nevermodel, from the bare bones

A finance system that reads evidence, drafts a forecast, and asks you to confirm before anything counts. The interface should feel like that: a clean sheet, a pencil draft, and ink once you sign off.

InkActuals, closed months, and anything you have approved. Full contrast. Solid marks.
PencilForecasts, projections, and unconfirmed facts. Grey, lighter weight. Erasable by design.
HighlighterThe one thing waiting on you. A yellow mark on the exact phrase, never a whole card.

Negative numbers are the only other colour, in red ink. There is no green, no blue, no gradient. Every colour on screen means something an accountant would recognise. The same eight tokens are defined once for light and once for dark, so the metaphor holds in both.

Sign in

Nevermodel owns the sign-in interface: a small, left-aligned 384px column centered on the page. The title carries the name rather than a wordmark. Better Auth supplies the local email/password session layer, while the form remains normal Nevermodel markup. Sign in and create-account states are explicit.

Type

Two families, clearly different jobs. Source Serif 4 for anything you read or sign: headings, the composer, extracted-fact prose. Inter for anything you operate or count: buttons, labels, metadata, tables, and every number. IBM Plex Mono, DM Serif Display, and Manrope are removed.

Colour

Eight tokens, each defined for light and dark. Greys carry a faint blue-violet bias so they read as chosen rather than default. The highlighter and red ink are semantic and appear only when their meaning applies. This page follows your system theme; switch it to see the dark set.

Theme is light, dark, or system, stored by next-themes as it is now. The control is a three-segment toggle in the account menu on the app page and in the bottom-right corner of the sign-in page. The current stylesheet declares every colour three times because it styles components inside theme blocks; the new one declares tokens twice and components once.

Spacing and shape

A 4px base. Vertical rhythm between blocks is 40 or 64, inside blocks 12 to 16. Radius encodes role: 8px for things you type into, 6px for small controls, 10px for the composer, a full pill for the one primary action.
